# Market Standards by Service Category

The evidence base for "thinking about the market update for this type of
service." When you recommend a change, cite the relevant standard here so the
recommendation reads as "meeting the current bar," not personal taste. These
are durable, widely-documented patterns rather than fads — but always confirm
against 2–3 current top competitors in the client's actual market before
presenting, and update this file when you learn the specific category.

## Cross-category baseline (applies to almost every service landing page)

- **Mobile is the primary surface.** The majority of service-business traffic is
  mobile; judge and design mobile-first, with a sticky action bar
  (call / WhatsApp / book).
- **One page, one primary action.** Landing pages convert best with a single
  dominant conversion goal repeated down the page, not a menu of options.
- **Speed is conversion.** Slower pages lose conversions and rank worse; Core
  Web Vitals ("good": LCP ≤2.5s, CLS ≤0.1, INP ≤200ms) are the accepted bar.
- **Specific, attributable proof.** Named testimonials with real outcomes,
  quantified results, recognizable logos, and real photos beat anonymous praise
  and stock imagery.
- **Benefit-led, second-person copy.** Lead with the visitor's outcome, in their
  words; quantify claims; put proof next to the claim.
- **Low-friction first step.** A free/low-risk entry offer (trial, quote,
  assessment, consult) outperforms a high-commitment ask.
- **Measurement by default.** GA4 + conversion events, and ad pixels if paid
  traffic runs. Untracked pages can't be optimized.
- **Trust & compliance basics.** Real contact/address, privacy policy, and for
  regulated fields the required credentials; consent for tracking where law
  requires (e.g., LGPD in Brazil).

## Academia / gym / fitness studio

- Sell the **outcome and experience** (transformation, energy, community, class
  vibe), not a feature list of equipment.
- **Booking / free-trial-class as the primary CTA**, WhatsApp-first for
  scheduling; sticky mobile CTA.
- **Authentic photos/video of the real space, classes, and members** — stock
  gym imagery reads as fake and erodes trust.
- **Transparent or at least anchored pricing / plans**; hiding price adds
  friction for a considered local purchase.
- **Social proof: member results, Google rating, class schedule, instructor
  bios** with credentials.
- **Location, hours, map, and neighborhood** front-and-center for local intent;
  LocalBusiness schema.

## Course / infoproduct / online academy

- **Clear promise: the transformation and who it's for**, with a concrete
  outcome and timeframe.
- **Curriculum/modules, instructor authority, and student results** with names
  and numbers; video testimonials convert strongly.
- **Risk reversal** (guarantee, free module/lesson), transparent pricing and
  payment options; scarcity/urgency only if genuine.
- **Checkout friction minimized**; mobile-first; fast.
- **FAQ handling the top objections** before the buy button; FAQ schema.

## Clinic / health / wellness / aesthetics

- Trust and **credentials are paramount** (professional registration, before/
  after where permitted by regulation, safety, hygiene).
- **Easy booking** (online scheduling / WhatsApp), location, hours, insurance/
  payment info.
- Reassuring, human imagery of real practitioners and space; MedicalBusiness /
  LocalBusiness schema.

## Agency / B2B service / consultancy

- **Outcome + proof-led**: case studies with measurable results, recognizable
  client logos, clear service scope.
- One clear CTA (book a call / request proposal); short qualifying form.
- Positioning against alternatives; a point of view, not a commodity pitch.

## Local service (trades, beauty, restaurants, etc.)

- **Click-to-call / WhatsApp as the hero action**; sticky on mobile.
- **Google reviews/rating, service area, hours, price ranges**, real photos.
- LocalBusiness schema, Google Business Profile alignment, fast mobile load.

## SaaS / software

- Crisp value prop + product screenshot/demo above the fold; benefit-led.
- Free trial / demo as primary CTA; social proof (logos, G2-style ratings).
- Transparent pricing tiers; security/compliance trust markers.

## How to use this file in the audit

1. Classify the page's category (state your reasoning).
2. Pull the cross-category baseline **plus** the category block above.
3. Sanity-check against 2–3 current leading competitors in the client's real
   market (search them; note what they do that this page doesn't).
4. For each recommendation, name the standard it satisfies. That citation is
   what makes the PDF defensible as a "market update," not an opinion piece.
